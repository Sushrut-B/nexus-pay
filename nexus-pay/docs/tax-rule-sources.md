# Tax Rule Sources & Versions

## ITD-FY2025-26-v1 (FY 2025-26 / AY 2026-27)

Sources:
- Income Tax Department e-Filing portal: "Tax Slabs for AY 2026-27" — `incometax.gov.in/iec/foportal/help/individual/return-applicable-1`
- Income Tax Department: "Threshold Limits under Income Tax Act", AY 2026-27 (as amended by Finance Act 2026) — `incometaxindia.gov.in/w/threshold-limits-under-income-tax-act`

Rules implemented:
- New regime (115BAC(1A)): nil up to ₹4,00,000; 5% 4–8L; 10% 8–12L; 15% 12–16L; 20% 16–20L; 25% 20–24L; 30% above 24L. Standard deduction ₹75,000. Rebate 87A up to ₹60,000 for total income ≤ ₹12,00,000 with marginal relief. Surcharge 10/15/25% at >₹50L/1Cr/2Cr (25% cap above ₹5Cr). Cess 4%.
- Old regime: nil up to ₹2,50,000; 5% 2.5–5L; 20% 5–10L; 30% above 10L. Standard deduction ₹50,000. Rebate ₹12,500 for total income ≤ ₹5,00,000. Surcharge 10/15/25/37%. Cess 4%.

## ITD-FY2024-25-v1 (FY 2024-25 / AY 2025-26)

Sources:
- Income Tax Department e-Filing portal: "Tax Slabs for AY 2025-26" — `incometax.gov.in/iec/foportal/help/individual/return-applicable-3`
- Press Information Bureau: Budget 2024 standard deduction increase to ₹75,000 for the new regime.

Rules implemented: new regime nil up to ₹3L, 5% 3–7L, 10% 7–10L, 15% 10–12L, 20% 12–15L, 30% above 15L; rebate up to ₹25,000 for total income ≤ ₹7L. Old regime unchanged.
